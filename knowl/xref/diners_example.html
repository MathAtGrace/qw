Exercise 1.

A group of diners were asked how much they would pay for a meal. Their responses were: $7.50, $25.00, $10.00, $10.00, $7.50, $8.25, $9.00, $5.00, $15.00, $8.00, $7.25, $7.50, $8.00, $7.00. $12.00.
  1. Find the mean, including units.
  2. Find the median, including units.
  3. Based on the mean and the median, would you expect the distribution to be symmetric, skewed left, or skewed right? Explain.
Solution.
  1. In Excel:
    =average(7.50,25.00,10.00,10.00,7.50,8.25,9.00,5.00,15.00,8.00,7.25,7.50,8.00,7.00,12.00)
    \(=\$9.80\)
    There are 15 times shown, so \(n=15\text{.}\) The mean is:
    \begin{align*} \bar{x} \amp= \frac{(7.50+25.00+10.00+10.00+7.50+8.25+9.00+5.00+15.00+8.00+7.25+7.50+8.00+7.00+12.00)}{15}\\ \amp=\$9.80 \end{align*}
  2. In Excel:
    =median(7.50,25.00,10.00,10.00,7.50,8.25,9.00,5.00,15.00,8.00,7.25,7.50,8.00,7.00,12.00)
    \(=\$8.00\)
    There are 15 times shown, so \(n=15\text{.}\) We start by listing the data in order:
    $5.00, $7.00, $7.25, $7.50, $7.50, $7.50, $8.00, $8.00, $8.25, $9.00, $10.00, $10.00, $12.00, $15.00, $25.00
    \(Median=\$8.00\)
  3. Since the mean is greater than the median, we would expect the distribution will be skewed right.
